ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Welcome to Min Secondary School

Min Secondary School is located in the heart of Tharpu Bazar, Myagde-4, Tharpu, Tanahun, Gandaki Province, Nepal.

We are committed to providing quality education from Nursery to Grade 12 in a supportive and student-friendly learning environment. Our aim is to develop knowledgeable, responsible, confident and capable individuals.
